Prayer Widget 1.1.2 adds a choice of notification duration in Settings.

- Choose **Auto dismiss after 10 seconds** (default) or **Keep visible until dismissed**.
- The selected notification duration is saved across restarts and applies to all iqama reminders, including tomorrow's Fajr.
- Includes the GoPray fetching fixes from version 1.1.1.

Download the Windows setup executable below, quit Prayer Widget from its tray menu, and run the installer to update. Existing installations do not support automatic app updates; this release must be installed manually.
